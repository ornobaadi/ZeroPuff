ZeroPuff

Smoke-free habit & streak tracker · Android · by Silio Digital

ZeroPuff helps you build a smoke-free habit. Log your cravings, check in daily, follow your smoke-free streak, and see how many cigarettes you have avoided and how much money you have saved. Celebrate milestones along the way.

ZeroPuff is a habit-tracking and motivation app. It is not a medical app and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
