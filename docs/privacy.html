Privacy Policy

App: ZeroPuff (package name com.zeropuff.app) · Developer: Silio Digital · Last updated: 30 September 2026

In short: You can use ZeroPuff as a guest and nothing leaves your device. If you sign in with Google, your habit data is backed up to our cloud so you can restore it. We do not show ads, do not sell your data, and you can delete your account and all data at any time inside the app.

1. Who we are

ZeroPuff is published by Silio Digital ("we", "us", "our"). We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy. You can contact us at aadi4789@gmail.com.

2. What ZeroPuff is (and is not)

ZeroPuff is a habit and streak tracker. It calculates how long you have been smoke-free, how many cigarettes you have avoided and how much money you have saved, and sends reminders you choose. It is not a medical device or health service, and it does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice.

3. Information we handle

3.1 Guest mode (no account)

You can use the app without signing in. In guest mode all information you enter is stored only on your device. We do not receive it.

3.3 What we do not collect

  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data
  • Health-sensor, fitness or wearable data (ZeroPuff does not connect to Health Connect or any health platform)
  • Advertising ID or data for advertising
  • Payment information (the app has no purchases)

4. How we use information

  • To provide the features of the app: tracking, calculations, reminders, backup and restore
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • To fix problems and improve the app (using the app activity events above)
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your information for advertising, do not build advertising profiles, and do not make automated decisions that affect you legally.

5. Where information is stored

  • On your device: the app always stores your data locally first, in a local database and app preferences.
  • In the cloud (signed-in users only): supported data is synced to our backend provider, Supabase, which hosts our database and authentication. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections, and database access rules restrict each account to its own records.

6. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us run the app, and only as needed:

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of users and the app. The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

7. Notifications

If you allow notifications, ZeroPuff schedules reminders (daily check-in, streak protection, milestones, and your chosen smoking-window alerts) locally on your device. No push-notification service or third-party messaging service is used. You can turn reminders off in the app (Profile → Reminders) or in your Android system settings.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Guest data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud data is kept while your account is active.
  • Deleting your account inside the app (Profile → Delete account) permanently deletes your account and all associated cloud data immediately, and erases local data on your device. You can also request deletion by email; see our account deletion page.
  • After deletion, routine encrypted backups kept by our hosting provider are overwritten on their normal rotation schedule, typically within 30 days. We do not restore or use them.
  • We may retain limited information only where the law requires it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures: encrypted connections, per-account access restrictions on the database, and signing keys kept private. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us right away.

10. Your choices and rights

You can at any time:

  • View and edit your habit information and logs in the app
  • Turn reminders on or off
  • Sign out, or use the app as a guest
  • Delete your account and data (in the app or by email)

Depending on where you live (for example, in the European Economic Area, the UK, or California), you may have additional rights, including to access, correct, port, restrict or object to processing of your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. To exercise these rights, email aadi4789@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

11. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

We process your information to perform our agreement with you (providing the app and your account), based on your consent where you sign in and enable optional features such as reminders, and for our legitimate interest in keeping the app secure and improving it.

12. Children

ZeroPuff is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the providers' standard contractual clauses.

14. Medical disclaimer

ZeroPuff is a tracking and motivation tool. Estimates such as cigarettes avoided and money saved are based on the information you enter. Nothing in the app is medical advice. If you need support with quitting, or have any health concern, speak to a qualified professional or a local quitline.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. If we make material changes we will notify you in the app or by other appropriate means.
