Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains how ZeroPuff (package com.zeropuff.app), published by Silio Digital ("we", "us"), handles your information. ZeroPuff is a habit and streak tracker. It is not a medical app and does not provide medical advice.

1. Using the app as a guest

You can use ZeroPuff without an account. In guest mode, everything you enter stays on your device and is not sent to our servers.

2. Information we collect

DataWhenWhy
Google account name, email address and profile pictureOnly if you sign in with GoogleTo create your account and let you back up and restore your progress
Habit information you enter: quit date, cigarettes per day, pack size and price, currency, usual smoking times, your personal reason for quittingDuring setup and when you edit your profileTo calculate your streak, cigarettes avoided and money saved, and to time reminders
Logs you create: smoking logs, craving logs and outcomes, daily check-ins (mood, notes), achievementsAs you use the appTo show your progress and history
Notification preferencesWhen you change reminder settingsTo schedule reminders you asked for
Basic in-app activity events (for example, that a craving session was started or completed)Signed-in usersTo understand how features are used and to improve the app

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or device advertising ID. We do not show ads and we do not sell your data.

3. Where your data is stored

  • On your device: your data is always stored locally first.
  • In the cloud (signed-in users only): supported progress is backed up to our backend provider, Supabase, so you can restore it on another device. Sign-in is provided by Google Sign-In.

4. How we use information

To provide the app's features (tracking, reminders, backup and restore), keep your account secure, and improve the app. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, ZeroPuff schedules reminders locally on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in your system settings.

6. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app (Supabase for storage and Google for sign-in), or when required by law. We do not sell or rent personal information.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your cloud data until you delete your account. You can permanently delete your account and all backed-up data at any time inside the app (Profile → Delete account) or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Deletion removes your data from our servers.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and access to backed-up data is restricted to your account. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

ZeroPuff is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data in the app, or contact us to make a request. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy law.

11. Medical disclaimer

ZeroPuff is a tracking and motivation tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Speak to a qualified professional for medical concerns.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
