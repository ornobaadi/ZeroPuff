Delete your ZeroPuff account

You can delete your ZeroPuff account and data at any time.

Option 1: Inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open ZeroPuff and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and all backed-up data are permanently deleted, and local data is erased from the device.

Option 2: By email

If you can no longer access the app, email YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL from the email address linked to your Google account with the subject "ZeroPuff account deletion". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, profile picture)
  • Smoking logs, craving logs, daily check-ins and achievements
  • Notification preferences and in-app activity events

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We keep no copy of this data afterwards, except where the law requires us to.

Guest mode

If you never signed in, your data exists only on your device. Use Profile → Delete local data or uninstall the app.
